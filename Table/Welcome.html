These all charge for entrance. Advance booking online at these places is often cheaper, also you can often use your student ID card to get a concessionary price.

Visit Coventry Transport Museum right here in the centre of Coventry. https://www.transport-museum.com


Visit Midlands Air Museum at Baginton (village on edge of Coventry) https://midlandairmuseum.uk/
